Premium Smart Restaurant Management Dashboard (AI-Powered)
Design a premium, modern, AI-powered Restaurant Management Dashboard for restaurant owners, managers, chefs, and staff.
This dashboard is completely separate from the customer website and should feel like a professional command center for managing restaurant operations in real time.
The interface should combine the elegance of Apple, the organization of Notion, the analytics of Stripe Dashboard, and the clean layout of Linear.
The design should prioritize clarity, fast decision-making, real-time information, and operational efficiency.
Everything should have smooth animations, reusable components, responsive layouts, premium charts, modern cards, and beautiful data visualization.

Overall Experience
The dashboard should feel like an AI-powered restaurant operating system, not just a CRUD admin panel.
It should instantly communicate that the manager can oversee every aspect of restaurant operations from a single place—orders, reservations, queues, inventory, staff, customers, sales, and AI recommendations—all connected in real time with the customer-facing application.
The final design should be visually impressive, highly interactive, and polished enough to feel like a commercial SaaS product suitable for presentation at a national-level hackathon.
